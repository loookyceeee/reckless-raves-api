Spin the Wheel rules

Reckless Spin the Wheel is a free promotion run by Reckless Raves, Belfast.

How to enter

  • Sign up with your email address for one free spin.
  • One free spin per email address. No purchase is needed to enter.
  • Paid spins are switched off.

Prizes

  • 10% off Reckless Raves tickets.
  • 20% off Reckless merch.
  • One free ticket to the next Reckless Raves event.

Most spins land on NOTHING. The 50% OFF and RESPIN wedges on the wheel are not prizes.

How results work

  • Every result is decided by our server after you sign up, not by where the wheel looks like it stops.
  • Prize codes stay hidden until you claim them with your email.
  • Codes are single use, so screenshot yours.
  • Ticket codes are entered at checkout on Skiddle. Merch codes are entered at checkout on recklessraves.co.uk.
  • A free ticket is for the next Reckless event only.

Your email

Your email is used to give you your spin and your code. See our privacy policy.
